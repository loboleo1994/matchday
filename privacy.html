Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Matchday ("we", "us") is operated by Leonardo Lobo. Contact: landrettalobo@gmail.com. This policy explains what information the Matchday app handles and your choices.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide: your account details (such as name and email address), profile photo, preferred positions and self-rated attributes, the groups you create or join, attendance confirmations, and match records such as results, goals, assists and MVP votes. Messages you send to support are also kept.

Approximate location: if you allow it, we collect your approximate location (for example city or area, not precise GPS) so you can find groups and matches near you.

Information collected automatically: device identifiers, how you use the app (for example screens viewed and features used), and crash and diagnostic data. We use analytics and crash-reporting tools from third-party providers to understand usage and fix problems.

You can sign in with email and password, Google or Apple. If you use Google or Apple sign-in, we receive the basic profile details they share with us, such as your name and email address.

2. How we use information

To provide the app (profiles, groups, matches, statistics, rankings, Elo ratings and honors), to show your information to other users as described below, to find nearby groups and matches, to keep the app secure, to analyse usage, to fix bugs and improve it, and to respond to your requests. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use your data to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

3. What other people can see

Your name, profile photo, positions and match statistics are visible to other members of the groups you belong to and may appear in rankings. Match records are part of a group's history, so some records may remain visible to the group after you leave.

4. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that host and operate the app on our behalf, and with authorities when required by law.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and personal data at any time by emailing landrettalobo@gmail.com from the address linked to your account.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Brazil's LGPD and the EU/UK GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data and to object to certain processing. Email us to exercise these rights.

7. Children

Matchday is intended only for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your data, but no system is completely secure.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version here with a new date.
