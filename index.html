Why Matchday exists

Football is part of the weekly routine for millions of people: pickup games, company teams, indoor leagues, synthetic turf tournaments. What stays with us isn't the glamour, it's the post-match conversation. Who won, who scored the most, who made the best assist, who disappeared in the big game.

The problem is that almost none of those stories are preserved. After a few weeks the debate turns into guesswork. And every time a message appears saying "Football is canceled, we'll try again next week", the group gets a little weaker.

Matchday gives amateur football groups the organisation, history and identity that professional sport has enjoyed for decades.

What you can do

Organise your games. Create or join groups, schedule matches and see who has confirmed.
Record every match. Goals, assists, MVP votes and final scores, including past matches, become your group's permanent history.
Build your legend. Track goals, assists, win rate, recent form and your Elo rating over time.
Climb the rankings. Leaderboards for goals, assists, G+A, MVP and win rate.
Earn honors. Badges and milestones for consistency, performance and commitment.
Connect. Add friends, find rivals and discover other groups.

Whether it's a Sunday league, a company team or a weekly pickup game, Matchday turns the conversation that used to depend on memory into history.
