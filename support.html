Frequently asked questions

How do I create or join a group?

Open the Groups tab to create a group and invite your players, or use "Join group" on the home screen when you have an invite.

How do I record a match?

Create the match in your group, then record goals, assists and MVP votes. You can also record a match that was already played.

Why don't I see my Elo rating?

Elo starts after your first closed match. Your statistics appear once a match with you in it has been recorded.

How do I edit my profile?

Go to the Profile tab and tap the edit icon to change your photo and positions.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email us from the address linked to your account asking for deletion and we will process the request. See our Privacy Policy.

I found a bug or have a suggestion

Email us with your phone model, app version and a short description. Screenshots help.
